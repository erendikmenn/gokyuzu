# Game assets: not covered by the code licence

The **code** of Gökyüzü is open source under the Apache License 2.0 ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). The **game assets** are not.

## What the assets are

The files the game loads from `assets/` at runtime:

- 3D models: aircraft, cockpits, airports, landmarks and city buildings;
- textures and images: aircraft, cockpit, facade and roof textures, the processed terrain imagery, menu art;
- audio: engines, rotor, wind, systems, warnings and voices;
- map tiles: terrain, water, city, tree and airport tiles and their indexes.

They are about 6 GB, generated by the scripts in `tools/` and `blender/`, and **they are not in this repository**.

Some material inside the assets is not the project's to reserve, and keeps its own terms:

- **Map data.** Where the map tiles contain data derived from OpenStreetMap or Overture Maps (building footprints and
  heights, tree positions, airport layouts, coastlines, land classes), that data remains available under the Open
  Database License 1.0: © OpenStreetMap contributors, Overture Maps Foundation. The small databases the game reads are
  committed in [`data/`](data/README.md) under the ODbL, and the scripts that make the tiles from the source data are
