## What it covers

The original assets made for Gökyüzü by Mehmet Eren Dikmen:

- 3D models: aircraft, cockpits, airports, landmarks and city buildings, generated by the project's Blender scripts;
- textures and images: aircraft and cockpit textures, facade and roof atlases, the terrain imagery as processed by the
  project, the menu and loading-screen art, the minimaps and screenshots;
- sounds: the synthesised engine, rotor, wind, system and warning sounds, and the voices and sound effects generated
  with ElevenLabs (see [below](#elevenlabs-sounds));
- map tiles: terrain, water, city, tree and airport tiles, with the ODbL parts described [below](#what-is-not-covered).

These files are not in git. They are built by the scripts in `tools/` and `blender/` and published as a separate,
trademark-free **asset pack** on the repository's GitHub Releases. To get it:

```sh
node tools/assets/fetch_pack.mjs     # downloads the pack from GitHub Releases and verifies its checksums
```

The few asset files that are committed are also under this licence: `src/ui/assets/*.jpg` (minimaps) and
`docs/perf/ref-2026-09/*.webp` (reference screenshots).

**Files from the live site.** For a fast start, `node tools/serve.mjs --assets-from https://fs.erenailab.com` fetches
the files the game asks for from the live site. Those files are not the asset pack and are not licensed by this file:
use them only to develop and test Gökyüzü, and do not redistribute them. They may include brand liveries that the pack
leaves out.
